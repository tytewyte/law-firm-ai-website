## 🎯 HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES:

### **STEP 1: Find Law Firms to Contact**

**Where to find them:**
- Google: "personal injury lawyer [your city]"
- Google: "family law attorney [your city]"
- Google: "immigration lawyer [your city]"
- Avvo.com (lawyer directory)
- Justia.com (lawyer directory)
- LinkedIn (search "attorney" + your city)

**What to look for:**
- Small to medium firms (1-10 attorneys)
- Have a website (you're selling website AI)
- Practice areas: Personal Injury, Family Law, Immigration, Criminal Defense
- Located in your state or nearby states

---

### **STEP 2: Gather Information**

**For each law firm, write down:**
- `[FIRM_NAME]` - "Smith & Associates"
- `[ATTORNEY_NAME]` - "John" (first name only)
- `[PRACTICE_AREA]` - "Personal Injury" or "Family Law"
- Their email address (usually on their website)

---

### **STEP 3: Customize the Email**

**Open:** `marketing-cold-outreach.html`

**Replace these 3 things:**
1. `[FIRM_NAME]` → "Smith & Associates"
2. `[ATTORNEY_NAME]` → "John"
3. `[PRACTICE_AREA]` → "Personal Injury" (appears twice)

**That's it! Only 3 things to change!**

---

### **STEP 4: Send the Email**

**Easy method (same as before):**
1. Save the file after editing
2. Open it in Chrome/Edge (right-click → Open with → Chrome)
3. Press `Ctrl + A` (select all)
4. Press `Ctrl + C` (copy)
5. Open ProtonMail → Compose
6. Click in email body
7. Press `Ctrl + V` (paste)
8. **To:** attorney@lawfirm.com
9. **Subject:** "Quick question about [Firm Name]'s website"
10. Send!

---

### **STEP 5: Follow Up**

**If no response after 3-5 days:**
1. Open `marketing-follow-up-2.html`
2. Replace same 3 variables
3. Send to same attorney

**Subject:** "Following up - [Firm Name]"

## 🎯 BEST PRACTICES:

### **DO:**
- ✅ Personalize each email (use their actual name and firm)
- ✅ Send during business hours (9am-5pm their timezone)
- ✅ Follow up 2-3 times if no response
- ✅ Be professional and helpful
- ✅ Respond quickly when they reply

### **DON'T:**
- ❌ Send generic "Dear Sir/Madam" emails
- ❌ Spam the same firm multiple times per day
- ❌ Make false promises
- ❌ Be pushy or aggressive
- ❌ Give up after one email
