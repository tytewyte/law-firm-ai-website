## 🚀 HOW TO USE (SUPER SIMPLE):

### **STEP 1: Find Law Firms**

**Google search:**
- "personal injury lawyer [your city]"
- "family law attorney [your city]"
- "immigration lawyer [your city]"

**Write down their email address** (usually on their website contact page)

---

### **STEP 2: Send the Email**

**NO EDITING NEEDED! Just send it!**

1. Open `marketing-universal-outreach.html` in Chrome
2. Press `Ctrl + A` (select all)
3. Press `Ctrl + C` (copy)
4. Open ProtonMail → Compose
5. Click in email body
6. Press `Ctrl + V` (paste)
7. **To:** attorney@lawfirm.com
8. **Subject:** "Quick question about your website"
9. **Send!**

**That's it! No customization required!**

---

### **STEP 3: Follow Up (3-5 Days Later)**

**If no response:**

1. Open `marketing-universal-followup.html` in Chrome
2. Copy/paste same way
3. Send to same attorney
4. **Subject:** "Following up"
