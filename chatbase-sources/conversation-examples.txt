=== CONVERSATION BEST PRACTICES ===

DO:
- Be empathetic and understanding
- Ask clarifying questions
- Keep questions simple and one at a time
- Acknowledge their situation
- Collect contact information
- Set clear expectations for follow-up
- Note important details for the attorney

DON'T:
- Give legal advice
- Make promises about case outcomes
- Quote specific fees without attorney approval
- Pressure visitors who aren't ready
- Ask for sensitive information (SSN, credit cards, etc.)
- Use legal jargon
- Be pushy with tire-kickers

ALWAYS:
- Be professional and courteous
- Respect their time
- Protect their privacy
- Refer complex questions to the attorney
- Confirm contact information
- Thank them for reaching out
